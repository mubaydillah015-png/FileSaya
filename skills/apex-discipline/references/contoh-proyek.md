## Naskah

```
Motivation will leave you. Probably tonight.

It is a spark. It burns hot, then it dies.
Cristiano Ronaldo did not build his career on feelings.
He was a skinny kid from Madeira. Too thin, they said. Too weak.
At fifteen, a heart problem almost ended everything.
He did not wait to feel ready.
He trained when the stadium was empty.
He slept on schedule. He ate with purpose. He recovered like it was his job.
Nobody claps for that.
When you are alone, at your lowest, motivation does not show up.
Discipline does.
It is not a mood. It is a floor you build, one boring day at a time.

So stop waiting to feel it.
Do the work anyway.
The summit isn't given. It is earned. Stay disciplined.
```

## Yang diambil dari video referensi pengguna

Caption satu kata di atas, judul tebal di tengah, satu kata kunci berwarna aksen, latar gelap dengan cahaya samar di belakang objek, ganti scene 2–4 detik mengikuti ketukan, angka berjalan (counter). Yang **tidak** diambil: ilustrasi vektor, ikon, maskot, logo, isi.
